# Social background artwork

These original, text-free backgrounds were generated with the built-in image tool using the owner's screenshots as composition references. They are bundled for local automatic rendering, not downloaded on each post. No original agency branding or policy text remains.

- skyline.jpg: Toronto skyline at left, pale maple leaves, red silk wave at bottom.
- phone.jpg: seated man and laptop emerging from a phone on the right; white copy area at left.
- woman.jpg: woman reviewing papers in an office, Canadian flag and dark headline area.
- student.jpg: student reviewing a document at a laptop, library and Canadian flag inset.
- flag.jpg: red field and folded Canadian flag at upper right.

Prompt direction: preserve each reference's composition and photographic treatment; remove all text, logos, badges and contact details; reserve clean space for separate deterministic typography. Source image outputs were inspected before conversion to JPEG (supported by the bundled SVG rasterizer). All final logos, headlines, dates and contact actions are separate local layers.
